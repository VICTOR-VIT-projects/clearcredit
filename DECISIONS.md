| 2026-10-06 | Relay sends **200-cell batches** (contract cap stays 300) | Measured ~23.3k gas/cell (300 cells ≈ 7.1M). But EIP-7825 (Fusaka) caps a transaction at 2^24 = 16,777,216 gas, and gas *estimation* for 300-cell calls probed above that cap and was rejected by the node (seen while seeding 10 large real projects). 200 cells ≈ 4.8M gas: 3.5× headroom under the cap, and estimation succeeds. The 10 failed relays resumed with the same idempotency keys: each registered with exactly one registerProject tx; a third full seed run returned 33 replays and 0 new transactions. |
| 2026-10-06 | Seed boundaries invalid at hash precision are **snapped to the 1e-6° grid** in `build_claims` | After the independent security review added a check that the micro-degree-quantized (hashed) geometry is itself valid, VCS799 (simplified from 12,607 vertices) was rejected: valid in float, self-touching at ~11 cm. The check is correct, so the data was fixed, not the check: GEOS `set_precision(1e-6)` + `make_valid`, recorded in the claim's `boundarySource`. Area (2,058 ha) and forest cover (594 ha) unchanged; evaluation results byte-identical; all 30 real claims register on the v2 contract. |

| 2026-10-09 | **Relay must see its own writes before deciding or reporting** (`Chain._await_project`) | Base Sepolia's load-balanced RPC served stale reads.
